For outside quants: how funding works

If you run a strategy and want capital behind it, this is the route. Four steps, and none of them asks you to put in your own money.

1

A conversation

What the strategy does, where it trades, what its capacity is, what stopped working along the way and how you noticed.

2

Verification

A read-only API key to the account, or an export from the exchange's own interface with the account ID visible. We pull the fills ourselves and compute the metrics from raw executions. A file prepared by the team is not evidence.

3

Forward test

The strategy runs on our infrastructure, as a black box if the team prefers.

4

Allocation

Size set by capacity, risk limits agreed, the team's share written down. Size grows with results.

Two paragraphs are enough to start

A question, a partnership, or a strategy you want funded. Say what it is and where it trades. We reply within a few days.
